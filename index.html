Spanish Fluency recipe

Welcome to my personal roadmap to Spanish fluency, a recipe to become fluent in Spanish using the best tools available, or the tools that just work

Ingredients

  • Attitude
  • Time
  • Consistency
  • Language Transfer app
  • Anki app
  • Optional: Glossika Spanish Fluency 1/2/3
  • Optional: The Great Courses: Spanish I/II

Instructions

  1. The ultimate instruction for any tool: pause after question to answer.
  2. Start with Language Transfer, it has 90 very short but great lessons, perfect for beginners. Recipe: exactly one lesson daily, with proper pausing after each question.
  3. As you begin to grasp Spanish start using Anki: add your cards or find something within shared decks. Recipe: daily.
  4. Optional: Get and complete The Great Course Spanish I. Recipe: 10-15 videos monthly with completed workbook activities.
  5. Optional: Get and complete The Great Course Spanish II. Recipe: 10-15 videos monthly with completed workbook activities.
  6. Optional: Get and complete Glossika Fluency 1 audio course with GSR method within 104 Days. Recipe: exactly one lesson daily, with proper pausing after each prompt.
  7. Optional: After Fluency 1 get and complete Glossika Fluency 2 audio course with GSR method. Recipe: exactly one lesson daily, with proper pausing after each prompt.
  8. Optional: After Fluency 2 get and complete Glossika Fluency 3 audio course with GSR method. Recipe: exactly one lesson daily, with proper pausing after each prompt.
